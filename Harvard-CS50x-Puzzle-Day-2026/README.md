# CS50x Puzzle Day 2026 Solutions

Solutions and detailed approaches for all puzzles from Harvard CS50x Puzzle Day 2026.

This repository documents how each puzzle was solved step by step, including reasoning, intermediate insights, and final answers.

---

## Overview

CS50 Puzzle Day is a collection of logic, pattern, and multi-step reasoning challenges.  
This set includes:

- 10 puzzles (including a final meta puzzle)
- A mix of static and interactive problems
- Concepts ranging from cryptography and wordplay to graph traversal and constraint solving
