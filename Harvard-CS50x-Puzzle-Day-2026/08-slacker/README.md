# The Slacker

## Problem
This puzzle is a diagramless crossword with no predefined grid structure.

We are given:
- A blank grid with no marked black squares
- A full set of crossword-style clues
- Flavor text hinting at reading direction

The goal is to construct the grid and extract a final answer.

---

## Approach

### 1. Start with Confident Clues
Since the grid is empty, begin with clues that are easiest to solve.

Process:
- Identify answers with high confidence  
- Place them tentatively in the grid  
- Use their lengths to guide placement  

These initial entries act as anchors.

---

### 2. Build the Grid Structure
As more answers are placed:
- Intersections help confirm correct placements  
- Conflicts reveal incorrect assumptions  
- Black square positions are inferred from word boundaries  

This is an iterative process of fitting and adjusting.

---

### 3. Complete the Crossword
Continue filling the grid until:
- All clues are placed  
- The structure is consistent  
- Intersections align correctly  

At this point, the full diagramless crossword is solved.

---

### 4. Extract the Final Answer
The flavor text instructs reading diagonally from top-left to bottom-right.

Reading along the main diagonal reveals:
WORKS WORKMANSHIP

---

## Solution
WORKMANSHIP

---

## Notes
- Diagramless crosswords require both solving clues and constructing the grid  
- Early correct placements are critical for stability  
- The diagonal extraction is the final step, guided by flavor text
