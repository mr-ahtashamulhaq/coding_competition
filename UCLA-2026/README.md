# 🏆 About The Contest

This repository contains our accepted solutions from the **Open Division** of **CodeSprint LA 2026**, UCLA ACM ICPC's annual competitive programming contest.

CodeSprint LA is a 5-hour ICPC-style algorithms competition where teams compete to solve difficult programming problems under intense time pressure. The contest featured multiple divisions including Beginner, High School, and Open Division. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

We participated in the **Open Division**, the hardest track of the event, designed for experienced competitive programmers. The Open round contained **12 problems**, and our team successfully solved **7 of them** during the contest.

The competition was held on:

- **Saturday, May 9, 2026**
- **9:00 AM – 5:00 PM PDT**
- Organized by **ACM ICPC at UCLA** :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

# 🔥 Competition Experience

The Open Division problems were intentionally designed to be difficult even for experienced competitors. The contest followed traditional ICPC scoring rules where:

* Teams solve problems under strict time limits
* Wrong submissions add penalties
* Ranking depends on solved count + penalty time

What makes this repository interesting is that these solutions were written during a live contest environment with limited time, pressure, debugging, and strategy decisions.
